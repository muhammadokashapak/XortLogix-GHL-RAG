# Top 5 GoHighLevel Updates

 [ GO HIGH LEVEL UPDATES ] November 12, 2025 • 2 min read # Top 5 GoHighLevel Updates You Need to Know

 

GoHighLevel has rolled out a series of powerful updates that are designed to transform how agencies and businesses manage automation, CRM, AI, and client experiences. Whether you’re running multiple brands or building advanced workflows, these features help your agency work smarter, faster, and more efficiently.

 

Watch the full video below, then read the summary for key insights.

 

## Custom Objects for Real Business Logic

 

With the new Custom Objects, you can model your business workflows directly inside GoHighLevel. This feature allows agencies to represent complex client interactions, track detailed data relationships, and automate processes tailored to their unique needs. By structuring your business logic in a precise way, your operations become smoother and more adaptable to growth.

 

## AI Workflows and Agent Assists

 

The AI Workflows and Agent Assists bring intelligent automation to the platform. Repetitive tasks can now be handled automatically, while AI suggestions help optimize your processes. This not only saves time but also allows your team to focus on strategic work, delivering faster results and better client experiences.

 

## Multibrand Capability

 

Managing multiple brands or clients is now seamless with Multi brand Capability. Each brand can have its own workflows, dashboards, and settings, all under a single account. This feature makes it simple for agencies to scale operations, reduce confusion, and maintain consistent quality across all business lines.

 

## High-Level Marketplace Evolution

 

The High-Level Marketplace has evolved to provide more integrations, tools, and resources than ever before. Agencies can easily discover new apps and solutions that enhance workflows, expand platform functionality, and simplify operations. It’s a central hub for tools designed to empower growth and efficiency.

 

## Revamped Reporting and Dashboards

 

GoHighLevel’s Reporting and Dashboards have been completely redesigned to deliver clearer insights. Customizable dashboards let agencies track KPIs, monitor pipeline performance, and measure automation outcomes more effectively. With this upgrade, teams gain the data they need to make informed decisions and drive measurable results.

 

### Conclusion

 

With these updates, GoHighLevel becomes an even more powerful tool for agencies. From AI-enhanced workflows to multibrand capabilities and improved reporting, these features streamline operations, save time, and improve client delivery.
