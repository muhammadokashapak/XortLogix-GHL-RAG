## 🔑 Key Features in Detail

 

Here’s what you can do with the new GBP Optimization feature:

Integrating your Google account is fast and seamless. Connect once, select your business profile, and HighLevel automatically pulls in all your data — giving you a complete and accurate foundation to start optimizing.

 

### ✅ Update Business Info, Hours, and Descriptions

 

Keep your business details accurate and up-to-date. You can edit your name, phone number, address, hours, and descriptions directly inside HighLevel. Accurate info builds trust and helps your business rank higher in local searches.

 

### ✅ Upload Photos and Services to Boost Visibility

 

HighLevel allows you to upload images, add or update services, and highlight special offers. Active, visually-rich profiles attract more clicks and perform better in Google Maps and local search results.
