# GoHighLevel Quiz Update

 [ GO HIGH LEVEL UPDATES ] December 02, 2025 • 3 min read # GoHighLevel Quiz Update: Map Results Directly to CRM Fields for Smarter Automation

 

GoHighLevel quizzes have long been one of the best methods for generating leads. They excite curiosity, boost interaction, and provide you a greater understanding of your audience. However, there has always been a significant drawback: what happens to the data after the test is turned in.

 

Most users had to rely on:

 

- messy tags
- manual spreadsheets
- branching workflows with conditional logic
- external tools like Zapier or Make
- complicated pipeline workarounds

 

That system worked — but only barely. It was fragile, difficult to scale, and impossible to analyze properly for segmentation.GoHighLevel has just released one of its smartest updates to date:👉 Quiz results can now map directly into CRM custom fields automatically.

 

▶️Watch step-by-step in the video:

 

## What Changed — And Why It Matters

 

When a lead completes your quiz, GoHighLevel now automatically:

 

1. Calculates the total score or performance tier
2. Detects category-wise points or percentages
3. Identifies the highest-scoring category (your lead’s top interest or strategy)

 

Then, all of that data is pushed straight into CRM custom fields.

 

As a result, each contact record gains significance.You now have insights rather than an email address and a name:

 

- What they care about
- What they are ready for
- What they need next

 

Additionally, you may use the data anywhere since it is saved directly inside the CRM:

 

- workflows
- smart lists
- emails
- automation
- triggers
- reporting
- segmentation

 

No duplication.No missing data.No inconsistencies.

 

## How to Enable CRM Field Mapping

 

HighLevel made the setup surprisingly simple:

 

1. Go to your Quiz Builder
2. Open the Results Page Customizer
3. Toggle Map scores into CRM custom fields

 

Here you can also choose how results will appear:

 

- Percentage
- Score out of 10

 

Once enabled, GoHighLevel automatically generates:

 

- fields with numbers (scores)
- text fields (labels or categories)

 

The CRM updates the same fields each time a lead retakes the test.

 

No duplicates.

 

No cleanup.

 

Just clean, structured data.

 

## A Real Example: The Ideal Marketing Strategy Quiz

 

To make this update real, let’s walk through a practical use case.

 

We built a quiz called The Ideal Marketing Strategy Quiz, designed to help businesses identify which focus benefits them most:

 

- Brand Awareness
- Lead Generation
- Customer Retention

 

Each quiz question contributes points to one or more categories.

 

🔹 Example:

 

- A user who values visibility = more points to Brand Awareness
- A user who wants steady sales = more points to Lead Generation
- A user obsessed with existing customers = more points to Retention

 

Once submitted:

 

- The total score is obtained.
- Individual category points are calculated
- The category with the highest score is identified.

 

And then — effortlessly — those values are sent to the CRM:

 

- brand_awareness_score
- lead_generation_score
- customer_retention_score
- top_category

 

This turns a simple quiz submission into a complete customer profile.

 

## Dynamics: Automation That Actually Personalizes

 

Once quiz fields exist in your CRM, you can use them inside processes to offer tailored content automatically.

 

Example:

 

📌 If top category = Brand Awareness→ Send branding strategy emails, visibility tips, and content frameworks.

 

📌 If top category = Lead Generation→ Trigger funnels, ads-based strategies, and conversion-focused resources.

 

📌 If top category = Customer Retention→ Deliver upsell strategies, loyalty nurturing, and retention automation.

 

And this is just the beginning.

 

Imagine:

 

- upsell logic
- course recommendations
- product suggestions
- service tiers
- coaching packages
- onboarding tracks
- personalization scorecards

 

All driven by the answers your lead already provided.

GoHighLevel’s quiz → CRM field mapping is more than a quality-of-life improvement.It’s a shift in how you personalize automation.

 

It transforms quizzes from:📩 lead magnet →⚙️ data engine
